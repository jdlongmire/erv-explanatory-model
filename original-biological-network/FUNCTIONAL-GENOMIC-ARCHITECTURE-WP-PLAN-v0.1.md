# Whitepaper plan: Functional Genomic Architecture

**Version:** 0.1 | **Date:** 18 November 2026

**Purpose:** Evaluate the evidentiary significance of demonstrated functions in noncoding and ERV-derived DNA for the Original Biological Network Hypothesis (OBN) within Designed Functional Maturity (DFM).

**Central thesis:** Experimental discoveries of functional viral-derived regulatory elements establish biological feasibility for sophisticated genomic architecture and motivate a design-friendly initial-state hypothesis. They do not independently demonstrate original placement, intentional design, or a date of integration.

**Audience:** Scientifically literate readers, comparative historical biology reviewers, design-friendly research community.

**Structure:** Executive summary; scope and definitions; primary evidence; design-friendly causal model; competing historical interpretations; prospective discriminating tests; limitations and falsification; conclusions; primary references.

**Review gates:**
1. Verify primary sources and distinguish experiments from author interpretation.
2. Distinguish biochemical activity, causal function, fitness contribution, and historical origin.
3. Include strong conventional co-option and common-descent accounts.
4. Register predictions independent of ERV case selection and lineage boundaries.
5. Do not claim the genome is universally functional or that function establishes design.
6. Preserve parallel research-programme independence and branch-only status.

**Deliverables:** Standalone Markdown whitepaper v0.1 and this plan, on `research/biid-erv-origin`. No main merge.
