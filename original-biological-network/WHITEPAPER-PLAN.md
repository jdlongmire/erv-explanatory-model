# Whitepaper development plan

**Project:** Original Biological Network Hypothesis
**Status:** Working plan, 18 November 2026
**Branch:** `research/biid-erv-origin`

## Purpose
Develop a scientifically literate, explicitly Designist account of an originally functional biological information network, with a bounded claim of explanatory plausibility rather than demonstrated superiority or proof of origins.

## Audience and thesis
Readers familiar with genetics, evolutionary biology, philosophy of historical science, and Designed Functional Maturity. Thesis: present ERV-like structures need not, as a matter of logic alone, all originate from prior infection; an initially functional genomic and ecological architecture is a candidate historical explanation requiring mechanistic and genomic constraints.

## Whitepaper architecture
1. Executive summary and epistemic scope.
2. Observations, mechanisms, historical reconstructions, and initial-condition premises.
3. Designed Functional Maturity and the radiogenic-heat-budget analogy.
4. Original network functional architecture and potential failure modes.
5. Intra-kind diversification and cross-kind viral transmission.
6. ERV evidence classes: family homology, orthologous insertion, flanks, target-site duplications, shared mutations, present function.
7. Rival explanations and explanatory burdens.
8. Human/non-ape positive, other-ape negative search protocol.
9. Plausibility gates, limitations, and revision triggers.
10. Primary-source bibliography and research agenda.

## Evidence protocol
- Primary sources only for specific empirical claims; DOI verification is required before publication.
- Separate empirical HIGH confidence from historical UNCERTAIN hypotheses.
- Never infer ancestral benefit from present benefit alone.
- Do not equate morphological distance with independently demonstrated created-kind boundaries.
- Do not conflate family-level similarity with an orthologous insertion.
- Record negative evidence, incomplete assemblies, deletion, incomplete lineage sorting, independent integration and contamination as competing explanations.
- Keep independent parallel programmes firewalled, except permitted primary data and citation corrections.

## Milestones
**WP v0.1:** coherent argument and sourced empirical anchors; no new genomic result claimed.
**WP v0.2:** verified locus-level evidence matrix and independently sourced accession/assembly metadata.
**WP v0.3:** preregistered comparisons and quantitative plausibility assessment.
**Review gate:** owner review before any PR or merge to main.

## Acceptance criteria for v0.1
Complete abstract, explicit premises and claims, strongest objections, actionable study design, properly qualified conclusions, and bibliography. Existing BIID history remains untouched.
