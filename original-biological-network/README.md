# Original Biological Network Hypothesis

Working research folder for the Original Biological Network Hypothesis within the Designed Functional Maturity framework.

## Research objective

Assess whether an originally functional biological information network, potentially involving beneficial viral systems and regulated genetic exchange, offers a plausible account of observed ERV-like genomic structures and their distribution.

## Scope and safeguards

- Consider original genomic architecture, diversification within created kinds, cross-kind viral integration, inheritance, and subsequent modification or degradation as candidate mechanisms.
- Distinguish observed sequence features and experimentally demonstrated processes from historical interpretations and foundational premises.
- Examine shared ERV families, orthologous insertions, shared mutations, and functional elements separately.
- Prioritize the proposed human-positive, non-ape-positive, other-great-apes-negative signature as an unresolved research question; no qualifying locus has yet been verified.
- Preserve the prior BIID research under `research/biid/` as historical work. Do not silently transfer findings or conclusions from independent parallel programmes.
- This folder records a provisional hypothesis, not an established account of viral origins.
