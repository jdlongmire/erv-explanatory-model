# Cross-lineage ERV evidence review: post-Fall transfer permitted

**Date:** 18 November 2026. **Status:** Primary-literature screening; no independent assembly validation.

## Research question
Are there documented endogenous retroviral signatures shared (A) by humans and a non-ape mammal but absent from other great apes, or (B) by nonhuman apes and a non-ape mammal but absent from humans?

The Original Biological Network permits *later* cross-kind viral transmission, including transmission after the hypothesized Fall. Therefore, a positive distribution does not require initial placement, and the timing and causal origin are distinct subsequent questions. The Fall is a theological/historical premise and is not itself dated or established by these genomic observations.

## Taxonomic reporting rule
Record **non-ape primates (monkeys)** separately from **non-primate mammals**. The former meet the literal 'non-ape' label but provide a weaker cross-lineage comparison than the latter. Operational created-kind boundaries remain undefined and must not be inferred from the ERV result.

## Findings and boundaries

**Verified from primary publication reports:** Family-level ape/Old World monkey positives with human negatives for PTERV1 and CERV2; ERV-Fc cross-mammalian distribution; specific human/chimp insertion-versus-deletion classifications.

**Not verified:** Any complete A or B signature requiring a **non-primate mammal** positive and all stipulated ape exclusions; any exact orthologous cross-mammal insertion; the date or origin of a putative post-Fall transmission.

**Research consequence:** Focus subsequent searches on species-level exclusion for particular ERV subfamilies, then inspect loci. Avoid treating an entire broad family as a qualifying signature when other apes also carry it. Both original placement and later viral transmission remain permitted causal paths in the model; the distribution alone does not discriminate them.

## Next screening gate

For every candidate, require (1) exact signature/subfamily definition, (2) independent evidence of presence in at least one non-primate mammal, (3) all stipulated ape presence/absence statuses with assay/assembly sensitivity, (4) locus or family-level classification, and (5) alternative explanation and source provenance. An untested species is **unresolved**, not negative.
