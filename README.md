# ERV Explanatory Model

Research repository for competing explanatory models of endogenous retroviral (ERV) observations.

## Research governance

Hypotheses are provisional. Distinguish primary evidence, derived findings, conjectures, predictions, and falsification criteria. Preserve provenance and avoid presenting speculative origin histories as established findings.

Research tracks should be developed on dedicated branches before review and incorporation into `main`. Independent parallel research programmes must retain their previously agreed information boundaries.

Initial track: `research/biid-erv-origin` (Biological Information Infrastructure Degradation).
